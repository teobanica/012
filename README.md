# Quantum permutations and the Standard Model algebra

**Teo Banica and Claude Opus**, November 2026

The finite part of the Standard Model, in the Chamseddine–Connes approach, has algebra A_F = ℂ ⊕ ℍ ⊕ M_3(ℂ). This paper is the outline of a program, whose aim is to understand the Standard Model in terms of quantum permutations, by using the noncommutative geometry approach of Chamseddine and Connes, and the twisting methods for quantum permutation groups.

## Main results

- The quantum automorphism group of the complex algebra B_F = ℂ ⊕ ℂ ⊕ M_2(ℂ) ⊕ M_3(ℂ) generated by A_F, with its canonical trace, is a cocycle twist of S_15^+. With the trace coming from the fermions, the coaction preserves each summand, and the quantum automorphism group becomes a twist of S_4^+ *̂ S_9^+, with algebra C(SO_3) * C(PU_3^+).
- The quantum subgroup G_3 ⊂ S_9^+ preserving the oriented area tensor on F_3^2 is a twist of the real color group PU_3. It is infinite and noncommutative, with classical version F_3^2 ⋊ SL_2(3), of order 216. The quantum symmetry group of the unoriented area tensor is classical, equal to F_3^2 ⋊ GL_2(3), of order 432.
- The Lie algebra gl_3 is the twist of the color Lie algebra ℂ[Z_3^2], with bracket [e_g,e_h] = (1 − w^det(g,h)) e_(g+h). In the Weyl basis, the 8 gluon components are labeled by the nonzero elements of F_3^2, with trigonometric structure constants.
- Also: Weyl matrix models, Hopf images and maximality, the Heisenberg group of order 27 acting on the fermions, the bound of 3 color points with connected CKM mixing, the spectral action and classicality, the mixing matrices and complex Hadamard matrices, and a look into Yang–Mills theory.

## Running the code

The programs need Python 3 with numpy. Each of them runs on its own, for instance `python3 maradona.py`, and writes its results to the matching `-output.txt` file.
